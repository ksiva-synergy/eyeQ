EyeQ · Design system 03

Motion moments

Five reward moments, each under 1.2 s, one spring curve, one ease-out. Every full-screen moment is skippable by tapping anywhere and collapses to a 10 ms cross-fade under reduced-motion. Haptic: one light tap at the peak frame. No particle systems, no confetti — vector shapes only, so they run on low-end Android.

Rules every moment follows
Under 1.2 s, always. Celebrations never block the next action; the Continue button is live from frame 0.
Two curves only. Spring (.34,1.56,.64,1) for things that gain (flame, badge); settle (.2,.9,.2,1) for things that move (bars, rows, numbers).
Vector, not video. Shapes, strokes and opacity only; no blur, no particles, no GIF or Lottie heavier than 60 KB.
Skippable and quiet. Tap anywhere to skip; reduced-motion collapses everything to a cross-fade; sound off by default.
Never for failure. Streak-lost and paywall states get a 200 ms fade only. Motion is a reward, not a nag.
