EyeQ · Forge Your Peak · Design system 01

Colour tokens

Black ground, one violet primary, one red reserved for the single most important action on a screen. Every semantic colour has a fill (white or near-black text on it) and a text variant (4.5:1 on the ground). Status is never colour alone: it always pairs with a glyph or a label.

Violet is structure
Primary buttons, selected tabs, progress fills, links, level badges. Never for alerts.
Red is the one action
At most one red button per screen: Start session, Mark done, Buy. Red text only for destructive or loss states.
Ember and gold are rewards
Streak flame is ember; XP and coins are gold. They are the only warm colours, so earned things feel earned.
Flat by default
Surfaces are flat fills with 1 px hairlines; no blur, no glass, no photo backgrounds. Keeps a ₹10k Android at 60 fps.
